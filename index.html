Exercise 2: Live Radio Transmission

Complete the radio conversation between field agent Fox and hacker Byte. Choose must, can't, might, could, or should for each gap.

must 99–100% sure it is true

Strong evidence shows something is true.

The lights are on. Someone must be inside.
can't 0% — impossible

Strong evidence shows something is NOT true. (Use can't, NOT mustn't!)

The door is locked from outside. He can't be inside.
might / could 50% — possible

You are not sure; it is a guess.

It might / could be a trap.
should Expectation

You expect something to happen normally or soon.

The hack should take 10 seconds.
